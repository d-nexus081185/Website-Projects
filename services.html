Our services

Our Approach to Psychiatric Care

Psychiatric treatment is not one-size-fits-all. Your treatment plan may include psychiatric evaluation, diagnostic assessment, medication management, education, supportive interventions, and coordination with other healthcare professionals when clinically appropriate.

What we provide

Services

All appointments are currently both physical and virtual, through secure telehealth. An initial evaluation is required before treatment recommendations are made.

  • Psychiatric evaluation

    A comprehensive evaluation to understand your current concerns, symptoms, history, and treatment goals.

  • Diagnostic assessment

    A careful assessment to help identify potential concerns and determine an appropriate diagnosis and treatment plan.

  • Medication management

    When medication is recommended, it is approached thoughtfully, with attention to effectiveness, safety, potential side effects, interactions, and ongoing response.

  • Follow-up care

    Follow-up frequency is individualized to your treatment needs, medication changes, response to treatment, and clinical stability.

  • Patient education

    Clear information about your treatment options, so you can understand them and take part in decisions about your care.

  • Supportive interventions

    Supportive interventions may be included as part of an individualized plan, alongside evidence-based psychiatric treatment.

  • Care coordination, when clinically appropriate

    Coordination with other healthcare professionals, and referrals when therapy, additional specialty services, or a higher level of care would benefit your treatment. Traditional psychotherapy is not currently provided as a primary service.

Our approach

We consider the whole person—not just the symptoms.

Treatment may take into account

  • Mental health history
  • Medical history and current medications
  • Family and social history
  • Current symptoms and functional concerns
  • Previous treatment experiences
  • Individual goals and preferences
  • Cultural, personal, and spiritual values

When medication is recommended, treatment is approached thoughtfully, with attention to effectiveness, safety, potential side effects, interactions, and ongoing response.

Faith-informed care

Your values matter.

We respect the cultural, spiritual, and personal beliefs that shape each individual's understanding of mental health and healing.

For patients who desire it, care may incorporate faith-informed and meaning-centered support alongside evidence-based psychiatric treatment.

Faith-based conversations are always optional and guided by your preferences. Our goal is never to impose beliefs, but to respect and support the values that are meaningful to you.

Psychiatric services

Conditions we evaluate and treat

We provide comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and medication management for a range of mental health conditions, including but not limited to:

  • Depression and Mood Disorders
  • Anxiety and Trauma-Related Conditions
  • Attention and Behavioral Conditions
  • Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders
  • Psychotic Disorders
  • Personality Disorders
  • Sleep-Related Concerns
  • Substance Use and Co-Occurring Mental Health Conditions

01Depression and Mood Disorders

  • Major Depressive Disorder
  • Persistent Depressive Disorder
  • Bipolar Disorders
  • Other mood-related conditions

02Anxiety and Trauma-Related Conditions

  • Generalized Anxiety Disorder
  • Panic Disorder
  • Social Anxiety Disorder
  • Trauma-Related Disorders
  • Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
  • Adjustment Disorders

03Attention and Behavioral Conditions

  • Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)
  • Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD)
  • Other behavioral and emotional concerns when clinically appropriate

04Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders

  • Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
  • Related obsessive-compulsive symptoms and conditions

05Psychotic Disorders

  • Psychotic-spectrum conditions
  • Schizophrenia-spectrum disorders
  • Other conditions involving psychotic symptoms

06Personality Disorders

  • Borderline Personality Disorder
  • Other personality-related conditions when appropriate for outpatient psychiatric care

07Sleep-Related Concerns

  • Insomnia
  • Sleep difficulties associated with psychiatric conditions

08Substance Use and Co-Occurring Mental Health Conditions

Mental health and substance use can sometimes occur together and may influence one another. We provide psychiatric evaluation and treatment for individuals experiencing substance-use-related concerns, including:

  • Alcohol Use Disorder
  • Opioid Use Disorder
  • Stimulant Use Disorder
  • Cannabis Use Disorder
  • Co-occurring mental health and substance use conditions

When clinically appropriate and within the provider's scope of practice, medication-based treatment for substance use disorders may be considered as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.

Not every condition is appropriate for outpatient or telehealth treatment. When a higher level of care or additional specialty services are needed, appropriate referrals and care coordination may be recommended.

Appointments

Care in the setting that suits you.

All appointments are currently both physical and virtual, through secure telehealth.

Telehealth privacy and confidentiality

Telehealth appointments are conducted through secure telehealth technology designed to support confidential healthcare communication. Please join from a private and secure location whenever possible.

Is telehealth right for everyone?

Telehealth may not be suitable for every person or clinical situation. Suitability is assessed during your evaluation. If your needs call for a higher level of care or in-person services, an appropriate referral may be recommended.
