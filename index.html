A safe space to begin

You deserve care that listens, understands, and treats you as a whole person.

Starting psychiatric treatment can feel difficult, especially if you have never sought mental health care before. Whether symptoms are interfering with your daily life or you simply want to better understand what you are experiencing, a psychiatric evaluation can help identify potential concerns and explore appropriate treatment options.

California Is & Important Information

Psychiatric services are provided by a California-licensed, board-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner within the provider's scope of practice and in accordance with applicable California laws and regulations.

Services provided through this practice are subject to clinical appropriateness and availability. An initial evaluation is required before treatment recommendations are made. Not every patient or condition is appropriate for telehealth or outpatient psychiatric treatment.
